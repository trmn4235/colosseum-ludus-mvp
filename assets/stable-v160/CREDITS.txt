All six diffuse, displacement and roughness images are the ready-made 1K
source maps, recompressed to WebP without painting or generated replacement
pixels. The Three.js materials set the requested dark grey stone palette,
natural warm brown timber palette and metre-based texture scale. Wood grain
follows each post or beam. Height/roughness maps stay in linear data space.
Original download URLs and source/output hashes are recorded in sources.json.
