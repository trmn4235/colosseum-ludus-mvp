# Roma V148 — geography and generated travel artwork

## Geography

Map anchors are based on the official Roma Capitale tourist service, checked 21 October 2026:

| Anchor | Latitude | Longitude | Source |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Colosseum visitor/address point | 41.889373 | 12.492438 | [Turismo Roma — Colosseum](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/places/flavian-amphitheatre-colosseum) |
| Circus Maximus official POI | 41.886650681433466 | 12.484508661426391 | [Turismo Roma — Circus Maximus](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/places/circus-maximus) |
| Ludus Magnus | 41.890324 | 12.495008 | [Turismo Roma — Ludus Magnus](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/places/ludus-magnus) |

Circus Maximus is **one** venue in the valley between the Palatine and Aventine hills. See also [Sovrintendenza Capitolina — Circo Massimo](https://www.sovraintendenzaroma.it/content/circo-massimo-2).

The map uses north-up longitude/latitude projection over 12.474–12.498 E, 41.8800–41.8945 N, with a near-metric aspect ratio at Rome's latitude. The Colosseum footprint is centred on the amphitheatre rather than its visitor-address marker. The circus footprint follows the northwest–southeast valley axis; Palatine is northeast of it and Aventine southwest. The official circus POI is provided as a reference, not misrepresented as its geometric centre.

The two selectable circus markers are **game approach points**, near opposite sides of the one building: Palatine approach (41.8870,12.4863) and Aventine approach (41.8852,12.4840). They do not assert an excavated or historically proven ancient gateway. Both routes reach the existing hippodrome's shared ticket/visitor gallery.

The Tiber course, surrounding street texture, hill contours and small building blocks are schematic. This map is a game navigation illustration, not a survey or a precise ancient street reconstruction. The drawn route connects game destinations, not a verified archaeological road. Ludus Magnus provides the historical reference for the game's Ludus departure point.
